Their presence means "we took a snapshot"; absence means "we never touched this block". The value being `null` (key present, value null) means "user had no pre-existing value, and the key on the global was deleted by the installer's deep-merge path or by us on uninstall". This is the cleanest way to distinguish "user had no pre-existing value" from "we never touched this block" in a JSON-only manifest.
